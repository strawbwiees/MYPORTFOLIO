AROMA: Administrative Retail Ordering Management System

A web-based platform developed to modernize and digitalize the operations of Coffee Chapters, a study lounge cafe. The system allows customers to browse the cafe menu, place orders online, and make contactless payments. On the admin side, the system provides a cashier-facing order management panel and an inventory management module that enables staff to monitor stock levels, process orders efficiently, and generate sales reports.
